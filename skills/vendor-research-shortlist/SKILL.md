# Vendor research shortlist

## Inputs

Ask for the category and intended use, testable hard requirements, preferences, exclusions, geography and data needs, named candidates or discovery scope, ranking criteria, maximum finalists, and decision deadline. Use supplied answers before asking again. Resolve ambiguous hard requirements before filtering.

## Steps

1. Turn the requirements into a comparison checklist. Keep elimination criteria separate from preferences. A subjective requirement needs a defined evaluation task before it can count as met.
2. Find candidates within the requested scope. Read specific documentation or source pages for each relevant claim and record checked dates and account or regional conditions.
3. Mark each cell met, failed, or unknown with evidence. Unsupported claims remain unknown. Drop candidates with verified hard failures and retain the exclusion reasons.
4. Rank eligible candidates using the reader's criteria. Unknown hard requirements must stay visible and be confirmed before a decision depends on them. Return fewer finalists when evidence does not support the target count.
5. Write strengths, trade-offs, unresolved questions, and any held outreach questions. If requirements or the deadline change, recheck sources and filtering from the recorded candidate set.

## Approval boundary

Contacting vendors, creating accounts, starting trials, accepting terms, and sharing private requirements require the human's OK. Show the exact recipient, message, or form before approval. Paying, deleting, or publishing also requires approval. Stop affected research when the next step would disclose private information without permission.

## Output

Return a comparison table: candidate, each hard requirement's status, source URLs and checked dates, strengths, trade-offs, and unanswered questions. Add ranked finalists, dropped candidates with reasons, and proposed next decisions. Keep outreach held.

Append a receipt: criteria, candidates considered, sources checked, inclusion and exclusion reasons, failures, open questions, and any approved outreach with its result.

## Check against the brief

Confirm every hard requirement was evaluated, unknowns were not counted as passes, each finding has a source or gap, and finalists fit the requested scope and count. Check evidence freshness against the deadline. If research access is unavailable, name the missing checks instead of claiming a verified comparison.
