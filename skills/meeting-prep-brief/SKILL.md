# Meeting prep brief

## Inputs

Ask for the event or calendar, qualifying meeting rule, attendee addresses, purpose, relevant threads and notes, lead time and time zone, private destination, and disclosure limits for that channel. Ask for public background scope only when it helps the meeting. Use supplied answers before asking again.

## Steps

1. Confirm the event is current, with start time, time zone, attendees, and purpose. Match attendee addresses to thread context before using private relationship history. If identity is ambiguous, omit that history and flag the gap.
2. Read relevant notes and messages in chronological order. Extract promises, open questions, and changed circumstances; check later threads for resolutions.
3. Verify relevant public facts on specific source pages. Separate public background from internal history. Mark assumptions, unverifiable facts, and conflicts visibly.
4. Write one page with the likely decision or open question at the top. Check that sensitive notes are permitted in the requested channel.
5. Recheck the event before delivery. Refresh time-sensitive facts if the time or attendees change; label the version. If canceled, stop delivery and record the cancellation. Deliver only to the reader's named private destination.

## Approval boundary

Preparing and delivering the requested private brief needs no additional send approval. Sharing with attendees, sending a follow-up, changing the calendar, paying, deleting, or publishing requires the human's OK. Hold delivery if sensitive context cannot be safely included under the reader's disclosure rules.

## Output

Return one page: event and current start time, confirmed attendees, purpose, relationship history, promises and their status, open questions, relevant public facts, and unknowns. Link claims to notes, threads, or public sources.

Append a receipt: event version, identity checks, threads and notes, public sources and checked dates, gaps, brief version, and actual delivery time and destination.

## Check against the brief

Check identities, chronology, source links, one-page length, lead time, and privacy of the destination. Confirm resolved promises are no longer marked open. If calendar access, delivery, or scheduling is unavailable, report the gap and work from supplied records without claiming a future delivery is scheduled.
