# Press and podcast list

## Inputs

Ask for the story angle, target topics and audience, publication window, target count, exclusions, previous outreach list and minimum contact interval, approved product facts, tone, and acceptable public contact routes. Use supplied answers before asking again.

## Steps

1. Find candidates from relevant recent pieces and episodes, rather than a broad list of names. Verify the original author or host and outlet. Drop ambiguous or syndicated bylines when the original author cannot be confirmed.
2. Check each person's specific piece, publication date, story fit, and public contact route on their own site, outlet page, or linked byline. Record source URLs and checked dates. Do not infer private email addresses.
3. Compare exclusions and past outreach before drafting. Drop unverified entries, out-of-window pieces, and contacts inside the exclusion interval, with reasons.
4. Draft a short pitch tied to a concrete point in each person's work. Use only approved facts. Omit unsupported quotes, results, or numbers.
5. Before an approved send, recheck the contact route, current outlet and work, and previous contact. A stale route or changed recipient stops that pitch and requires renewed review. Return fewer contacts when fewer meet the rules.

## Approval boundary

Research never authorizes outreach. The human must approve each exact recipient and pitch, or a batch showing all individual recipients and messages. Paying, deleting, or publishing also requires the human's OK. Keep research and drafting within the requested public sources and supplied facts.

## Output

Return a table: person, outlet, piece or episode and date, fit, public contact route, evidence links, checked date, previous contact, and eligibility. Include exact held pitches and a dropped log with reasons.

Append a receipt: visited pages, verification outcomes, exclusions, draft versions, edits, approvals, stale records, and actual send status and time.

## Check against the brief

Check every eligible entry has a verified person, recent work, story fit, public contact route, and no exclusion. Confirm pitch claims match supplied facts and the final count reflects verified entries. If sources cannot be reached, mark affected entries unverified and exclude them from the outreach-ready count.
